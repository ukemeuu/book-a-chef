# 30-Day Go-To-Market & Liquidity Strategy

**Goal:** Onboard 50 high-quality chefs in Nairobi in 30 days and guarantee at least 3 bookings per chef per month to retain supply.

## Phase 1: 30-Day Supply Acquisition (Onboard 50 Chefs)

### 1. Vetting Checklist (The "Standard")
To keep quality high and charge premium prices, chefs must meet these requirements:
- [ ] Active KRA PIN & National ID.
- [ ] Valid Food Handlers Certificate (Kenya Min of Health).
- [ ] Portfolio of cleanly plated dishes (Instagram or PDF).
- [ ] Passes a 15-minute Google Meet competency interview.
- [ ] *(Optional but preferred)* Formal culinary education or 3+ years restaurant experience.

### 2. Outreach Script & Strategy
**Target:** DM 200 food influencers and private chefs on Instagram (`#NairobiFoodie`, `#PrivateChefNairobi`).
> "Hi [Name], love your plating on that Swahili snapper dish! I'm launching ChefNyumbani, an exclusive platform connecting Nairobi's top private chefs with high-end clients. We’re selecting our first 50 founding chefs right now. You set your own prices, create your own menus, and we handle the bookings/marketing. Interested in securing an early spot?"

### 3. Incentive Structure (Early Adopters)
- **0% Commission for 90 Days:** The first 50 vetted chefs pay 0% platform commission on their first 10 bookings.
- **Free Professional Photography:** Offer a free 1-hour photoshoot for their top 3 menu items (High ROI for platform aesthetics).
- **"Founding Chef" Badge:** Display a permanent badge on their profile for trust signaling.

---

## Phase 2: Liquidity Strategy (Generating Demand to hitting 3 Bookings/Month)

If 50 chefs need 3 bookings each, we need to generate **150 bookings** per month.

### 1. The "Dinner Party in a Box" Corporate Strategy (B2B Focus)
Private clients are sporadic. Corporate budgets are consistent.
- **Action:** Pitch 20 tech startups and established firms in Kilimani, Westlands, and Upper Hill.
- **Offer:** "Friday Team Lunches" or "Client Entertaining at the Office". Instead of ordering standard delivery, a Chef nyumbani comes to the corporate kitchen/boardroom and plates a 3-course meal.
- **Goal:** Secure 5 corporate accounts that book weekly (20 bookings/month base load).

### 2. Micro-Influencer Launch Campaign
- **Action:** Gift 5 micro-influencers (Nairobi lifestyle/food creators) a free dinner party for 4 friends.
- **Requirement:** They must document the "ChefNyumbani experience" on TikTok/IG Reels (Chef arriving with groceries → cooking → serving → spotless kitchen).
- **Incentive:** Provide an affiliate code (e.g., `WANJIKU10` for 10% off). This acts as high-trust, word-of-mouth marketing driving high-intent leads to our top-rated chefs.

### 3. Subsidized "Date Night" Deals (Loss Leader)
Launch the platform with a highly marketable entry-level offer:
- **Product:** "The Ultimate KES 5,000 Romantic Date Night (For Two)".
- **Economics:** Chef charges KES 8,000. Customer pays KES 5,000. Platform subsidizes the remaining KES 3,000 as customer acquisition cost (CAC).
- **Distribution:** Market this specific package aggressively on Meta Ads targeting "Anniversaries", "Recently Engaged" in Nairobi.

### 4. Waitlist Matching & "Concierge" Service
For the first 3 months, if organic search isn't yielding results:
- Let customers fill a "Tinder-style" form (Date, Budget, Vibe).
- The Platform Admin manually pings 3 suitable, under-booked chefs via WhatsApp: *"I have a KES 25k dinner party lead for Friday. Who wants it?"*
- This manual matching ensures liquidity is distributed evenly so nobody churns out of frustration.
